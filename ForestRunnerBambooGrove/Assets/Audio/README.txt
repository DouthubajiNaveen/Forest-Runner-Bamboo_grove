AUDIO
All sounds are synthesised at start-up by Scripts/Audio/AudioManager.cs, so no audio files are required.
To replace a sound with a real recording, place an AudioClip in Assets/Resources/Audio/ using one of these names:
  click, coin, jump, slide, swish, land, powerup, shield_break, hit, gameover, splash, unlock, buy, denied,
  footsteps (loop), bird0..bird3, ambience_day (loop), ambience_night (loop), music_day (loop), music_night (loop)
A clip with a matching name overrides the generated one automatically.
